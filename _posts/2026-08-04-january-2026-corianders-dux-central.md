New Year, New RCCC?

With 2026 having just begun, the Starting Eleven of the RCCC's finest fellows ambled over the Dux Central to start the January visitation. While the Maharajah pushed his pennies into Japanese vending machine hoping for a pungent prize and others prayed for relief while on family holiday, pints were pounded and holidays tales swapped on the balmy Christchurch evening.

One can't fail to mention the infamous January 2018 debacle that occurred at Coriander's and we didn't go back for a number of years as a result. Thankfully things have improved and this visitation proved that they are right back on track. 

The Maharajah received a well deserved trophy in the OG CoTM along with a special note by Royal Decree detailing the level of his CoTMness (it was very high). Other trophies remained with RT (FOMO) and someone else.
